# Homes And Property: The Owner's Brief Of November 17, 2026, Item By Item

The persistent checklist for the owner's second housing brief ("City-by-city coverage requirement" and sections 1
to 11). A row is **done** only when the playable cycle works, its money and state agree and its failure paths are
tested; **partial** says what works and what doesn't; **not done** says why. The first package's 153 requirements
stay in `docs/homes/features.csv`; every place's coverage is in `docs/homes/places/`.
