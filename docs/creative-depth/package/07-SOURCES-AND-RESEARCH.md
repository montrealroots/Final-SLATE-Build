# Research Map And Evidence Rules

Checked for this blueprint on November 11, 2026:
- https://developer.themoviedb.org/docs/faq — API purpose, noncommercial/commercial distinction, attribution and third-party content notice. Free access is not a guarantee that every downstream use is cleared.
- https://developer.themoviedb.org/docs/image-basics — construct images from configuration, size and image path. Technical access is distinct from image rights.
- https://www.geonames.org/export/ — worldwide and country extracts; use its current attribution/license terms.
- https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing — structured main/property data CC0; other text and linked media can have different terms.

Implementation sources to consult (not claimed fully audited by this package):
- https://developer.themoviedb.org/reference/intro/getting-started — search/discovery, company, movie/TV credits, person, season and episode endpoints; verify current endpoints and filters.
- https://www.themoviedb.org/api-terms-of-use — full terms accepted by the owner. Existing repo records its interpretation; verify actual wording before public media/data deployment.
- Official Disney, NBCUniversal, Warner Bros., Sony Pictures and Paramount corporate leadership, subsidiary and newsroom pages. Verify date-specific ownership; never freeze current corporate structure from memory.
- Official national/regional film commissions and destination operators for site facts and filming guidance. Treat tourism information as discovery, not permit approval.
- GeoNames source documentation, Wikidata statements and references for geography and identity, official site pages for current operating details.
- Museum collection sites such as V&A, Smithsonian and local national collections for period costume/design references; verify individual object/image licenses and regional applicability.
- Official production notes and credited practitioner interviews for workflow evidence. Adapt processes rather than copying long creative text or claiming one studio workflow is universal.
- Existing repository awards rulebooks and child-rule source ledgers for applicable editions/jurisdictions; this blueprint supplies no new legal rate or deadline.

For each factual import retain URL, publisher, retrieval date, applicable period, field-level confidence and license/terms reference. Distinguish sourced, corroborated, partial, unavailable, simulated and original authored. A successful HTTP response is not proof of content accuracy. Do not fill blanks with guesses. Keep citations accessible without cluttering normal gameplay.

Do not use another film's plot as an original preset by changing names. The starter content in this package is original brainstorming material. Real genre definitions, historical references and venue claims require their own sources. The numerical game models should state units, intent and calibration; they are not represented as industry facts.
