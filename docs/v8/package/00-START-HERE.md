# SLATE — independent start, banking, budgets and industry overhaul (v8)

Prepared November 13, 2026, America/Chicago. Research, repository extracts and implementation design for Claude. No repository was modified. This package is not a finished game or a claim of exhaustive worldwide legal verification.

## Owner decisions and precedence
1. Start at age 19, independently, with actor/actress, screenwriter, producer and director available from the beginning. Taking all four roles does not grant expert ability or guarantee recognition.
2. Remove new-game family-class/support menus. Use one fixed fictional wealthy oil-family background. An inheritance is already released and liquid at the start; no family relationship or oil-business management subsystem is required.
3. Default design decision: USD 25,000,000 inherited cash, configurable before a new game. This amount is an original scenario choice, not a researched average or an owner-specified exact number. The setup screen states the amount before confirmation. No ongoing allowance, forced family royalty or unlimited bailout.
4. Start as an independent person, not automatically a registered studio. Allow a production banner and later optional incorporation. A banner is not proof of a separate legal entity or banking permission.
5. Enter a custom project budget. It is a spending plan, never newly created money. Separate target, estimate, funding commitments, received cash, reserved cash, paid costs, forecast-to-complete and proceeds.
6. Restore useful banking and paperwork depth from prior repos through adapters; do not transplant their old game framework, family formulas or tax engine.
7. Use one awards timeline per save. Future outcomes may change; no competing real-winner/simulated-winner cards in main gameplay.
8. Replace three-size first-look payments with scoped negotiated agreements. First-look is not the same as overall or a project greenlight. Private rates remain modeled, not claimed as a studio's published tariff.
9. Phone UI must be readable and responsive. This supersedes any older requirement to shrink a desktop canvas to phone width. Retain capabilities, content hierarchy and visual identity.
10. One agent. No subagents. No tutorial in this overhaul. Keep taxes excluded. All money and paperwork are simulated game records, never actual banking applications.
