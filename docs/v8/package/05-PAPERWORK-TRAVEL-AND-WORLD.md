# Worldwide paperwork integrated with production

## Scope and evidence
Use legacy destination data as a migration seed, not proof of current law. The old work table largely distinguishes performer work rights by citizenship and destination; it does not establish every filmmaker's self-employment, company-registration or sponsorship entitlement. Multiple passports, residence, role, activity, length, date and employer all matter. Unknown is not automatic permission or automatic refusal.

A UK Creative Worker source illustrates sponsorship and job restrictions; it explicitly does not allow starting one's own business under that route. An inherited fortune does not remove that limitation. Canada distinguishes work authorization from permission to enter; an exemption from one may not waive the other. The USCIS bulletin distinguishes motion-picture/television O-1B from arts assessment, not a blanket entry route for wealthy beginners. These are scoped examples, not a full legal guide.

## Document types
Identity/passport; residence status; entry visa or waiver; work permission; sponsor/employer documents; project filming/location permission; business registration; rights/license evidence; equipment customs paperwork when relevant. Bank verification and immigration documents may share facts, but approval for one is not approval for another. Use fictional identifiers only; no requirement for players to upload real documents.

Fields: holder, issuing jurisdiction, citizenship/residency basis, destination, route, permitted activities, sponsor/employer, project scope, valid from/to, entry limits, processing estimate, fee, refund rule, evidence version, application state and attachments represented as game records. Eligibility evaluation returns eligible/ineligible/needs-more-information/unknown with reasons and alternatives.

## Complete cycle
Plan activity/location/dates → evaluate citizenship/residence rights → identify applicable route or unresolved requirement → collect simulated documents → appoint appropriate in-world assistance where modeled → pay fees → submit → wait → respond to evidence request → decision → receive document → travel → perform only authorized activity → renew/change/exit → archive. Approval never follows merely because payment succeeded. A pending application does not authorize tomorrow's shoot.

Connect processing to calendar and project commitments. A delayed permit can move travel, cancel a refundable booking, incur a cancellation cost, replace a foreign worker with an available authorized local worker or move a location. Notify with the reason and costs. Advance must catch expiry before the unauthorized activity. Do not randomly create refusals unrelated to rules just to manufacture drama.

Use date-effective fees, currencies and exceptions; fees can differ by nationality, place of application, route and dependent. Processing estimates are uncertain and separate from legal validity. Some fees are nonrefundable. Do not invent a uniform worldwide visa charge or time. Do not reuse a tourist visit table to clear a commercial shoot.

## World and coverage registry
The old 252 identifiers include territories and special/historical entries. Preserve geographic keys while distinguishing country, territory and other status. Model issuer and destination relationships instead of pretending each key issues its own passport. Build a matrix for every key: banking identity/product availability, account eligibility, currency, passports, entry rights, work rights, permits, company registration, source dates and executable rule coverage.

Use explicit researched/legacy-unreverified/modeled/unknown/not-applicable statuses per field. Existing legacy “proven” rows may reflect no inhabited place rather than a completed financial system. Data can remain in the world map while legal workflows are marked modeled or unresolved. Keep technical source details in Credits and the developer ledger; use natural player messages such as “Your work permission does not cover this production.”

New game setup must not quietly infer citizenship solely from birthplace or ethnicity. Keep birthplace, citizenship, residence and language separate; rights depend on documented game identity. A player can choose lawful starting residence through scenario setup without being granted worldwide work rights.

## Research still required
The included legacy records are broad but not freshly revalidated worldwide. Full business-bank products, film-specific permits, complete work-route logic, citizenship/residence exceptions and historical 2026 fees remain country-by-country research. Route unknowns to an exact question list with affected fields and source attempts. Do not represent legacy coverage as new completion.
