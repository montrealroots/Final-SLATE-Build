# Nonplayable Roster Entries: Review

Every official award, section or program in `docs/awards/roster/` that the game shows but doesn't let the player enter, reviewed on
November 9, 2026: what it recognizes, whether SLATE has that activity, what is missing, the decision, and any conflict found.
The list is generated from the roster files' `- ` lines, so it is the roster as it stands; `tests/nonplayable-review.test.ts`
fails if an entry is missing here.

## What The Review Changed

Four reasons were false, and three of the four entries are now playable:

- **Emmys, Outstanding Narrator (Category 76)** said documentary narration had no credited narrator. A part whose story function
  is Narrator is cast and records its narration in post. Entered: the recipient is the cast Narrator of a documentary or nonfiction
  program with a narration sequence, judged on that performance (`emmys-2026@3`).
- **SXSW, Music Videos And XR** said music videos aren't made in the game. New Project offers the Music Video format. The Music
  Video Competition and its jury award are entered; XR stays out.
- **International Emmys, Sports Documentary** said sports weren't a documentary subject the game distinguishes. Sports is a genre.
  Entered, from the Sports genre.
- **Emmys, The Television Academy Honors** said the game doesn't record what issues a program takes on. A project can declare
  Social Issues. Corrected, and left out until the call for entries is read.

Two more reasons were corrected without entering anything: the Ustinov award's "the game's scripts are produced work" (a script
exists from its first draft) and the Annie anime category's reason (the game records the production country).
