# Performers must be employed under a SAG-AFTRA agreement (aa33-rules section One); union membership isn't modeled and
# is shown to the player as a simplification.
# Motion Pictures
male-lead | Outstanding Performance By A Male Actor In A Leading Role | C | lead:Actor | feature | aa33-rules One |
female-lead | Outstanding Performance By A Female Actor In A Leading Role | C | lead:Actress | feature | aa33-rules One |
male-supporting | Outstanding Performance By A Male Actor In A Supporting Role | C | supporting:Actor | feature | aa33-rules One |
female-supporting | Outstanding Performance By A Female Actor In A Supporting Role | C | supporting:Actress | feature | aa33-rules One |
cast | Outstanding Performance By A Cast In A Motion Picture | C | ensemble | feature | aa33-rules One | Every actor in the cast credits, not co-stars

# Non-Broadcast Stunt Honors
- Outstanding Action Performance By A Stunt Ensemble In A Motion Picture | Stunt performers aren't cast or credited separately in the game | aa33-rules One
- Outstanding Action Performance By A Stunt Ensemble In A Television Series | Stunt performers aren't cast or credited separately in the game | aa33-rules One
# Life Achievement
- The SAG-AFTRA Life Achievement Award | Also requires a history of humanitarian and public service work, which the game doesn't model | aa33-rules Two, Nine
