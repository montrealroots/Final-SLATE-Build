- Truer Than Fiction | Chosen by its own committee for an emerging documentary director; criteria not in the rules read | sa27-film Nominating Guidelines
- Producers Award | Chosen by its own committee for an emerging producer; criteria not in the rules read | sa27-film Nominating Guidelines
