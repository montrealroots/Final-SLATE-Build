# Official Selection (Article 3): In Competition, Un Certain Regard, Cannes Première, Special Screenings, opening and
# closing films. Films made in the previous twelve months, not shown outside their home country, not at another
# international festival, not online (Article 4). In Competition films must be released in French cinemas.
