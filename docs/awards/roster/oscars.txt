# Governors Awards
thalberg | Irving G. Thalberg Memorial Award | H | career-producer | feature | Rule 1.4a, Rule 25 | A creative producer whose body of work reflects consistently high quality
- Jean Hersholt Humanitarian Award | Humanitarian efforts outside production (Rule 1.4b) aren't modeled | Rule 1.4b
honorary | Honorary Award | H | career | feature | Rule 1.4c, Rule 25 | Lifetime achievement or exceptional contribution
# Special Awards
special-achievement | Special Achievement Award | H | career | feature | Rule 1.5a, Rule 26 | Not given every year
- Scientific And Technical Awards | Recognize technology development, outside the filmmaking the game models | Rule 1.5b, Rule 27
- Scientific And Technical Lifetime Achievement Award | Technology development isn't modeled | Rule 1.5c, Rule 28
- Scientific And Technical Service Award | Service to the Academy's technology work isn't modeled | Rule 1.5d, Rule 28
- Award Of Commendation | Technology development isn't modeled | Rule 1.5e, Rule 28
