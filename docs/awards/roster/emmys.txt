# The rules number every category 1 to 118. "Category" = nominees and one winner; "Area" = one, more than one or no
# winner; "Juried" = a panel decides, one, more than one or no winner (rules, Awards Definitions).

# Governors And Special Awards
legacy | Legacy Award | H | career-program | television | Legacy Award | At least 60 episodes across at least 5 seasons; chosen by the Special Awards Committee and the Board of Governors
governors | The Governors Award | H | career | television | The Governors Award | Chosen by the Special Awards Committee and the Board of Governors for transformational impact, not an overall body of work
- Syd Cassyd Founders Award | Recognizes service to the Television Academy, which the game doesn't model | Syd Cassyd Founders Award
- Outstanding Achievement In Engineering, Science & Technology | Recognizes engineering and technology development, outside the filmmaking the game models | Engineering award
- The Television Academy Honors | Recognizes programming that explores social and health issues; the game doesn't record what issues a program takes on | Television Academy Honors
- Bob Hope Humanitarian Award | Recognizes humanitarian work outside production, which the game doesn't model | Bob Hope Humanitarian Award
