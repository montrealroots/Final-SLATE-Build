# The 50th edition adds the VFX Award and widens the Art Direction Award to set decoration: 16 main awards (jap50).
# Eligible: theatrical fiction and animated films of 40 minutes or more first shown for paid admission in the Tokyo
# area from January 1 to December 31, 2026, at least three times a day in the first week and two weeks running in
# cinemas only, at one Tokyo cinema; new works made for theatrical release (jap50). Members vote the Excellence awards
# (the nominees) and then the best of each.
