# Chapter on the awards, Article 1 lists 25 categories; Article 2 the honorary César and the César des Lycéens.
# French-initiative feature films (CNC production approval, a national theatrical release in the previous year) compete
# for Best Film and the categories that follow from it (Article 3 A).
# Films
film | Meilleur Film | C | producer+director | feature:fr | Article 1, Article 3 A | The statuette goes to the delegate producers and the director
first-film | Meilleur Premier Film | C | producer+director | feature:fr:debut | Article 1, Article 3 K |
animated | Meilleur Film d'Animation | C | producer+director | feature:animated:fr | Article 1, Article 3 M |
documentary | Meilleur Film Documentaire | C | producer+director | documentary-feature:fr | Article 1, Article 3 L |
foreign | Meilleur Film Étranger | C | director | feature:non-fr | Article 1, Article 3 B | Films that don't meet the French nationality conditions; the statuette goes to the director
