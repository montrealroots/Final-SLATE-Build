# Recipient And Work-Specific Rules

Who may be entered for a work, and what the work itself must be, beyond its format, dates and release. Part 5 of the
awards request of November 9, 2026. Each row is one of: **applied** (read from the organizer's document, enforced,
tested), **modeled** (SLATE's own reading, said so where the player sees the rule), or **not modeled** (with the reason).

A licensed or pre-existing song, or one from a save made before songs were works (origin unknown), is refused with the reason. Judged on its writers' capability. | Five percent of the running time on screen | Emmys, guest and limited-series supporting performers | Applied (`emmys-2026@4`); **fixed** November 9, 2026 | `screenShare`, read from the cut (`screen-time.ts`): the minutes the player recorded the part on screen in each scene of the cut entered, over the episode entered (guests; their best when none is named) or the whole cut entered (limited and movie supporting). The game has no footage, so the time is the player's record, not a measurement | `tests/screen-time.test.ts`; `e2e/awards.spec.ts` (screen time journey, desktop and WebKit) |
