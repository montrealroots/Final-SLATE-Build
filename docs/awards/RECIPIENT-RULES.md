# Recipient And Work-Specific Rules

Who may be entered for a work, and what the work itself must be, beyond its format, dates and release. Part 5 of the
awards request of November 9, 2026. Each row is one of: **applied** (read from the organizer's document, enforced,
tested), **modeled** (SLATE's own reading, said so where the player sees the rule), or **not modeled** (with the reason).

| Rule | Organizer and section | Status | What SLATE does | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Narrators may not enter lead, supporting or guest categories | Emmys, Performer Awards (78th rules) | Applied (`emmys-2026@3`) | `noNarrators` drops a part whose story function is Narrator from every lead-, supporting- and guest- category, and says so | `tests/recipient-rules.test.ts` |
| Outstanding Narrator goes to the narrator of a documentary or nonfiction program | Emmys, Category 76 | Applied (`emmys-2026@3`) | The cast Narrator of a program with a narration sequence, judged on their own performance | `tests/nonplayable-review.test.ts` (a played documentary) |
| A guest is in fewer than half the eligible episodes | Emmys, Performer Awards | Applied (`emmys-2026@3`) | `episodeShare` reads the episodes each performer is credited in; until then the guest categories took whoever the producer declared a guest | `tests/recipient-rules.test.ts` |
| A short form performer is in half or more of the episodes | Emmys, Performer Awards | Applied (`emmys-2026@3`) | `episodeShare` | `tests/recipient-rules.test.ts` |
| Six episodes for series categories, three for documentary and hosted nonfiction | Emmys, Eligibility 11c | Applied (`emmys-2026@2`) | `minEpisodes` | `tests/rulebooks.test.ts` |
| Episodes of a set length: at least 18 minutes (Spirit), 20 (Actor Awards), more than 20, at least 8 for children's, under 20 for short form (PGA) | Spirit TV 3; Actor Awards B.2.b; PGA Series A.1.a, Specialty A.3, A.5 | Applied (`pga-2027@3`); **fixed** November 9, 2026 (`spirit-2027@2`, `actor-awards-2027@2`) | `episodeRuntime`: the delivered cut over its episodes, where the PGA's comedy and drama rule asks every episode. Until then the Spirit and Actor Awards floors read the whole series and refused nothing | `tests/rulebooks.test.ts` |
| Episode counts apply to series, not specials | PGA, "for series only" (Specialty A.3-5) | Applied (`pga-2027@3`) | `minEpisodes` skips formats that aren't episodic | `tests/rulebooks.test.ts` (a nonfiction special) |
| A televised or streamed motion picture has no theatrical release | PGA, T/SMP A.1 | Applied (`pga-2027@3`) | `tvFirst.never`: a cinema release anywhere, before or after streaming | `tests/rulebooks.test.ts` |
| Short form: at most six producers | Emmys, Category 86 | Applied (`emmys-2026@2`) | `recipients.max` | `tests/rulebooks.test.ts` |
| More than 50 percent non-English dialogue | Oscars, Rule Sixteen II.D | Applied (`oscars-2027@3`) | `nonEnglish` counts the approved script's words by each line's language; with no written dialogue, the declared first language decides | `tests/recipient-rules.test.ts` |
| More than 50% of the dialogue in a language other than English | Golden Globes, A.3.c.i | Applied (`golden-globes-2027@3`) | `nonEnglish`, as above | `tests/recipient-rules.test.ts`; `e2e/awards.spec.ts` (Non-English Language, desktop and WebKit) |
| Non-English Language goes to the directors and accredited producers | Golden Globes, A.3.i.ii | Applied (`golden-globes-2027@2`) | `recipients: director-and-producer` | `tests/rulebooks.test.ts` |
| A co-production has a co-production partner | European Film Awards, Eurimages award (criteria unread) | Modeled; **fixed** November 9, 2026 | An accepted co-producer deal from Financing, or a save's pre-financing co-production money. Until then only the second counted, so no partner signed in play qualified | `tests/recipient-rules.test.ts` |
| Original song | Oscars, Golden Globes, Emmys | Modeled | A scene's song not marked licensed is original; the credited composer receives the award, judged on the score's quality. SLATE has no separate lyricist role | `tests/awards-catalog.test.ts` (original song) |
| Craft awards go to the credited craft | Every organizer | Modeled | Credits whose role is the category's craft; judged on that craft's own quality where delivery recorded it | `tests/awards-catalog.test.ts` (craft recipients) |
| Honorary awards for a body of work | Oscars, Emmys, Annies, others | Modeled | Chosen by the organizer from a career at least ten years long with three delivered works; purposes outside production (humanitarian, service, technology) are not entered (`NONPLAYABLE-REVIEW.md`) | `tests/awards-catalog.test.ts` (honorary) |
| Dialects, pidgins and local English count as English | Golden Globes, A.3.c.ii | Not modeled | SLATE's languages don't record dialects | |
| A part performed as a character rather than a narrator goes to Character Voice-Over | Emmys, Category 76 | Not modeled | The Narrator function is the producer's declaration that the part narrates | |
| A performer nominated as lead or supporting can't later enter the same role as a guest | Emmys, Guest Performer Eligibility | Applied (`emmys-2026@4`) | `notAfterNomination`: an earlier season's (through `continues`) nomination, loss or win in a lead- or supporting- category for the same performer and part rules out every guest category | `tests/recipient-rules.test.ts` |
| Five percent of the running time on screen | Emmys, guest and limited-series supporting performers | Applied (`emmys-2026@4`) | `screenShare`: the approved script's scene minutes the part appears in, over its biggest episode's running time (guests) or the delivered cut (limited and movie supporting). | Directing and writing entered for one episode | Emmys, Categories 24, 25, 27, 29, 30, 113, 114, 116 | Applied (`emmys-2026@4`) | `oneEpisode`: the entry names an episode (Prepare Submission's Episode To Enter, else the first episode anyone credited worked on), and the award goes to those credited on it; comedy and drama directing to at most two. | A limited series entered as one part or whole | Emmys, Categories 26 and 115 | Applied (`emmys-2026@4`) | `oneEpisode.wholeWhenAll`: the complete series when everyone credited worked on every part, else one part chosen by the entrant; decided per entry, not per person, so a director of every part is also named on a part's entry | `tests/recipient-rules.test.ts` |
| A seven-day run in one of six US metro areas | Oscars, Rule Two 2c-d | Modeled | Releases are by country: a US theatrical release by a distributor stands for the run (`la-ny-theatrical` likewise) | `tests/rulebooks.test.ts` |
| A US co-production begun before production | Emmys, Eligibility | Not modeled | A co-production partner has no country in SLATE; the production base decides | |
| $150 million worldwide, $100 million of it in the US | Golden Globes, B.1.b | Applied (`golden-globes-2027@4`) | `boxOffice`: what distributors reported for theatrical releases dated in 2026; a release in several countries counts worldwide only. Not modeled: streaming viewership, and projections after November 21 | `tests/recipient-rules.test.ts` |
