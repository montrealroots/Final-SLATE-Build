# Realism contract

## 1. Four kinds of information
- Verified observation: a cited fact for a particular date, territory and edition.
- Derived value: a reproducible calculation from compatible observations; retain inputs and method.
- Simulation parameter: an explicit, versioned model choice used to make the game work.
- Player choice: a creative or business decision that may depart from history.
Never relabel one as another. Coverage reports expose missing and uncertain facts. Gameplay exposes uncertainty where it affects a decision, without flooding every screen with research notes.

## 2. The depth rule
For each feature and subfeature document: trigger; inputs; eligibility; choice; resources reserved; work performed; elapsed time; output artifact; validation; consequence; failure; recovery; later reuse. A subfeature is justified by a distinct decision or constraint, not an extra menu.

Every important choice must have an identifiable consumer. A costume era affects design research, wardrobe availability, continuity and potentially budget; it must not award a generic quality bonus. More spending changes resources, not guaranteed success. Interactions, bottlenecks and tradeoffs matter more than feature counts.

## 3. Existing foundation to preserve
Player begins with access to acting, screenwriting, producing and directing. Credits depend on work actually done; choosing not to act is valid. Multiple pronouns/identity choices, ethnicities and languages remain supported. Identity must not automatically determine ability, morality or success. Language proficiency may affect specific work requirements without substituting ethnicity for proficiency.

Keep geography, cities, local calendars, holidays, weather, birthdays, scheduled events, sleep and time advance. Preserve selectable 2026 start dates. Running clock is 1:1, closed game does not accumulate time. No manual play/pause control; an event decision may suspend advancement until resolved. Define open/background-tab behavior explicitly and test it against existing owner rules.

Preserve world coverage from the existing source repositories, including territories, without claiming that the requested roughly 252 geographic entries are all sovereign countries. Stable geographic identities matter more than a slogan count. No unrequested tax simulation or tutorial expansion.

## 4. Historical and playable truth
Historical ledger is immutable except versioned corrections. Playable timeline records player actions and alternate outcomes. Recorded real winners and grosses remain in historical views. If the player enters a historical award competition, playable results can diverge with an explicit timeline marker. Do not silently substitute simulated results into the historical table.

Realism does not require identical repeated outcomes, nor arbitrary novelty. Seasonality, competition and capacity produce variation; seeded uncertainty produces replayable variation; authored contextual text explains it. Do not force every year to have a different ceremony day if real dates or scheduling rules do not support that.

## 5. Boundaries and honest completion
Use public or otherwise authorized data and comply with provider requirements. Metadata access does not establish rights to reproduce full books, scripts, songs, recordings or images. Adaptation gameplay and music selection can use metadata and original concise descriptions; actual media inclusion requires appropriate permission. This is an implementation constraint, not a new in-game legal bureaucracy. Respect any later owner changes to simulated rights mechanics.

Do not invent real people's private salaries, personality, availability, negotiating preferences or endorsement. Simulated counterparts can have seeded availability and preferences labeled as gameplay. Skill ratings are future scope unless already implemented and specifically authorized; prepare interfaces without disguising reputation as talent.
