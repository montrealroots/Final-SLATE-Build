## How It Behaves

- **Not before it was published.** An observation is reportable from its published date, in the player's own calendar (src/engine/history.ts `reportable`, the v7 package's rule; until November 13, 2026 it waited until that day had ended where it was announced). An unknown publication date is never reported. A January 1 start sees the Golden Globes' nominees (announced December 8, 2025) and no 2026 winner.
- **Corrections.** A correction is a later revision of the same observation, with its own publication date; the earlier revision stays and applies before it (tests/history.test.ts).
- **Not in the save.** What a save shows follows from the data and the save's clock: a reload can't duplicate a result, and a later import can't rewrite what a save already settled.
- **Separate from the game's competition.** An edition's page shows the recorded results in their own card, with their source; the game's own entries and results stay under Your Entries.
- **Edition dates.** The 2026 Golden Globes (January 11), AACTA Awards (February 6), DGA Awards (February 7), Spirit Awards (February 15), César Awards (February 26), PGA and Goya Awards (February 28), Actor Awards (March 1) and Writers Guild Awards (March 8) and Japan Academy Film Prize (March 13) and Canadian Screen Awards (May 31; all ten from November 19, 2026) and Oscars (March 15) take their organizers' dates; from November 20, 2026 so do the 2026 Sundance (January 22 to February 1), Clermont-Ferrand (January 30 to February 7), Berlinale (February 12 to 22), Series Mania (March 20 to 27), Hot Docs (April 23 to May 3), Cannes (May 12 to 23), Annecy (June 21 to 27, moved for the G7 summit), Venice (September 2 to 12), San Sebastián (September 18 to 26), Busan (October 6 to 15) and Sitges (October 8 to 18). Until November 13, 2026 the Golden Globes' 2026 date was simulated (January 18 in Los Angeles). Saves made before then keep the dates they were made with.
