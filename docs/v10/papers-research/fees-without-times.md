# Fees Found Without A Decision Time

Researched November 17, 2026. Each fee below has a source, but no source found gave the route's decision time, so the
route stays on the game's estimate (`docs/v10/papers-estimates.json`) until one does.

- Mexico, visitor with permission for paid activities: consular fee USD 321 (Ley Federal de Derechos 2026,
  https://consulmex.sre.gob.mx/sanjose/images/PDFs/2026/LFD_2026.pdf); the INM's study of the employer's request
  MXN 4,182 (Deloitte, 2025). No resolution time found.
- South Korea, E-6-1: a single-entry visa of 91 days or more, USD 60 (https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/us-en/brd/m_4502/view.do?seq=715889).
  The recommendation and the visa issuance certificate's times weren't found.
- Norway, artist's residence permit: NOK 6,300 for an adult's work permit (secondary: https://dofmar.com/norwegian-residence-permit-cost/).
  UDI publishes waiting times only through its interactive guide.
- Iceland, work-based first permit: ISK 80,000 from January 1, 2026 (https://eiglaw.com/iceland-raises-fees-for-residence-and-citizenship-applications-in-2026/).
- Switzerland and Liechtenstein: fees are each canton's (Switzerland) or the Ausl-GebV tariff (Liechtenstein); no
  figure for an artist's short-stay permit was found.
- Kenya, Special Pass: KSh 15,000 a month (2023 Gazette review, https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2023/11/review-of-charges-and-fees-with-respect-to-immigra);
  one to two working days by one guide. Sources conflict with a newer USD schedule.
- Indonesia, C7 visit visa for arts and culture: Rp 1,500,000 PNBP for 30 days by the immigration page's summary
  (https://imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/C7); its time isn't on the page.
- Argentina, temporary residence for artists on an entry permit: the Permiso de Ingreso 100 UMSM and the temporary
  residence 100 UMSM for a non-MERCOSUR applicant (50 and 50 within MERCOSUR), at 1 UMSM = ARS 1,000
  (https://www.argentina.gob.ar/migraciones/cuadro-tasas-migratorias, Decreto 584/2024). No processing time given.
