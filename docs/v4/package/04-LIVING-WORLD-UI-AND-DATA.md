# Year-By-Year World, Catalogs And UI

## Historical observations and future simulation

Historical records carry entity ID, metric, unit/currency, territory, period, published/as-of date, source, revision and confidence/availability. Reveal information only on its appropriate in-game date. Keep observed, projected and simulated records distinct. Missing data is unknown, not zero.

Define the evidence boundary per title/territory/metric. Replay supported historical observations through their dates; beyond those observations simulate continuation. Do not double count overlapping periods. A save records its snapshot and boundary. Later imports do not rewrite settled player earnings or completed fictional decisions. Forecasts are not historical facts.

Provide a clear simulation policy: recorded history supplies external events and benchmarks, while the player's projects and resulting career decisions are fictional. A player's competing film cannot retroactively change a reported real receipt while the UI still calls it historical. In a deliberately diverging world, relabel affected outcomes as simulated. Never combine fixed history and counterfactual effects invisibly.

Research box-office and audience calibration from accessible suitable sources. Store definitions and samples, not untraceable coefficients. No promise that unpublished weekly ratings, private fees or all historical receipts can be obtained. Add manual authorized imports and dated sync reports where useful. A closed game does not secretly run network jobs.

## Box office and releases

Loop: marketing/distribution plan -> delivery/clearance -> territory/date/bookings -> screens/showtimes and capacity -> attendance -> ticket receipts -> exhibitor/distributor allocation -> guarantees/recoupment -> participant obligations -> player/company proceeds -> holdovers/expansion or withdrawal -> later windows -> history records and future offers.

Model admission caps, territory/currency, ticket price, release pattern, competition, marketing exposure, reception and word of mouth with bounded uncertainty. Preserve current balances through migrations. Weekly reports reconcile with settlement periods without assuming every calendar month is exactly four weeks. Distinguish opening weekend, opening week and lifetime; domestic/international/worldwide totals have compatible boundaries. Actual, simulated, nominal and adjusted records stay separately labeled. Rereleases and corrections update records once with traceability.

## TV and streaming

Broadcast viewers, household/demographic rating, share, hours, views, completion and chart rank are distinct measures. Require denominators for ratings/share and suitable data for demographics. Never transform a rank into invented viewers or label simulated data as Nielsen measurements.

Loop: platform agreement -> scheduling/availability -> promotion/reach/competition -> episode viewing -> retention/word of mouth -> weekly report -> contract-specific financial consequences -> commissioner/network/platform decision -> renewal, cancellation, rescheduling, final-season order or later-window deal -> next production commitments.

Differentiate weekly streaming, batch releases, linear TV, daily programs, live shows and limited series. Decisions use audience, cost, slate needs, contract options and delivered work, not one universal threshold. Allow audience growth and recovery as well as decline; avoid a formula forcing every episode to lose viewers. A renewal creates funding, writing, cast options, schedules and assets; cancellation handles outstanding obligations and future career options. Do not guarantee ratings-based pay where the agreement has a fixed fee.

## World freshness

The event engine carries state year to year: active shows, forthcoming productions, releases, project delays, renewed/cancelled seasons, talent work, corporate slates and evolving opportunities. Dates, causal prerequisites and resource limits govern events. Seed randomness for reproducibility, not identical annual resets. Persist commitments and identities; aging and career transitions must follow game dates.

Maintain event eligibility, cooldowns, contextual variants and memory of prior outcomes. Suppress redundant notices. Contextual variety should arise from different circumstances and decisions, not random renamed messages. Keep low-drama ordinary weeks possible. Do not force a company acquisition, artist scandal or other unsupported real-person event just to create novelty; simulated public events must be labeled and appropriate.

Expand international/independent companies, active shows, networks/services and cinema references with dated corporate relationships, role-specific operations and actual provenance. Each playable company needs capacity, strategy, proposals, contracts, payments and future consequences. A catalog-only reference must not pretend to be a functioning counterparty.

Reputation tracks craft by role, reliability, budget/delivery, audience, commercial results and relationships. Company outreach uses fit and need plus evidence, with explicit offer scope and negotiation. Include paid acting/writing/directing assignments and independent distribution, not only self-produced projects.

## Pictures and provenance

Retain full imported-catalog picture coverage: people portraits, title posters/backdrops, season posters, episode stills and available supported company/network logos. Track checked/downloaded/reused/unavailable/failed/excluded counts by kind, with stable IDs and deduplication. No promise that every entity has an image. Download All Available is optional with estimated/free space, verified shards, pause/resume/retry, update/removal, atomic installation and graceful offline fallbacks. Preserve progress across closure, not only an in-memory session. Keep images outside saves and startup bundles.

Validate current provider access, attribution, retention and redistribution requirements before changing ingestion. Preserve owner-only access and secrets protection. Put general bibliography and credits in Settings → Credits And Data Sources where allowed. Keep any required attribution in compliant locations. Do not claim Settings placement alone satisfies every provider. Game pages retain useful source title/creator and concise factual-versus-simulated labels without research clutter. No unauthorized music audio included with picture packs.

## Desktop and phone

Mirror desktop identity, section order, information, controls and actions on mobile. Reflow within phone width; do not shrink a desktop canvas or hide essentials. Audit every screen, tab, drawer, dialog, table, picker and loading/error/empty state, including new source, music and equipment flows.

Use shared spacing, typography, heights and corners. Fix overflow, bleed-through, overlaps, clipping, inconsistent alignment, z-index problems, sticky occlusion and layout shifts. Test long names, large catalogs, missing images, keyboard focus, zoom, reduced motion, touch targets, landscape and safe areas. Deliberate scrollable tables must not cause page-wide horizontal scrolling.

Compare equivalent states at desktop and phone widths; gameplay variance is not proof of UI differences. Inspect screenshots, not just bounding boxes. Track unvisited screens and real-device keyboard/accessibility checks honestly. No tutorial. Use established natural language/title-case rules without robotic filler.
