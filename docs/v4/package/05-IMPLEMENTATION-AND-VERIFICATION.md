# Implementation Order And Evidence

## Ordered milestones

A. Current-state reconciliation, superseded-quota removal, save migration and authoritative requirement ledger. Do not restart the build. Check current branch/CI before merging anything.
B. Source-first creation, whole-brief proposal/preview/apply/undo, editable characters and Guide Me mandates. Finish one film and one TV vertical slice before bulk enrichment.
C. Family resources, personal/project/company accounting, equipment catalog/shop/rentals and day reservations. Support an affordable independent short end to end.
D. Method-specific production graphs for live action, 2D, 3D, stop motion and hybrid. Connect revisions, locations, child-rule policy, travel and rest.
E. Source agreements and approvals, hired work/representation, composers/supervisors and the full score/song lifecycle.
F. Dated catalog expansion, historical evidence boundaries, territory box office, weekly audience and renewal/cancellation consequences, record books and career outreach.
G. Full media inventory and robust installs, complete desktop/phone UI cleanup, content provenance/credits and performance.
H. Final integration audits, whole-career journeys and exact-tree CI. Keep source ingestion and a bounded media fetch resumable while coding, without spawning agents.

For each milestone implement one complete loop, test it, document actual evidence, commit and push. Merge only after required CI passes. If another branch is active preserve its work and reconcile dependencies. Do not silently cut scope to finish a session. Keep precise resume steps at limits.

## Traceability matrix

Use requirements.csv supplied here as a starting inventory. Expand each row into child requirements for every introduced field, action, state transition, failure/recovery, consumer and test. No subsystem is done merely because its parent row is present. Fields: requirement ID, player action, saved state, prerequisite, reader, consequence, cost/time, failure/recovery, unit test, browser journey, factual/calibrated status, implementation state, evidence SHA. Track requested, implemented and verified separately.

Every mechanical input needs a comparison changing only that input, proving the intended consequence and stability of unrelated state. Test idempotency, budget limits, required approvals and reversal. Merely rendering a value is not proof that it affects play. Identity/reference/document fields need no fake numerical effect.

## Engine invariants and balance

Money conserves across accounts, project transfers, obligations and settlements. Gross is not player earnings. Events and retries charge once. Equipment and people do not double-book. A source change does not discard old payments or pending approvals. Permission scopes and release versions remain associated with the agreement they were approved under. Save refresh/removal does not rewrite careers.

Run seeds across resource levels, territories, formats and methods. Check credible failure and recovery, not just a successful path. Test positive audience growth, diminishing promotion returns, capacity ceilings and variable release timing. Calibrated balance targets must be documented; do not invent real-world precision. Evaluate repetitiveness with event-frequency and template-use reports plus human playtesting, not only average revenue.

Run relevant unit, integration and mutation tests; full dev and production Playwright suites on Chromium desktop/mobile and available WebKit iPhone profiles. Use fixtures for isolated cases, label them, and do not claim an entirely fixture-built career is a full playthrough. Physical iPhone and human accessibility checks remain outstanding until performed.

Measure startup, large-library search, full-brief application, long character lists, image loading/install/resume and long-career advancement, recording dataset/hardware and median/tail timing. Preserve budgets. Index/lazy-load content instead of shipping catalogs into startup JavaScript. Investigate intermittent failures, not merely raise timeouts.

## Research and delivery gates

Use official author/publisher/game sources for bibliography, credited release records for music and personnel, manufacturer documents for equipment, organizer rulebooks for awards and appropriate published metrics for historical results. These are research directions, not already-verified sources or a supplied price list. Verify current technical/API terms against official documentation. Record source date, scope, permissions and missing data. Search alternate authoritative sources where one is blocked; never manufacture data to satisfy a quota.

Final report includes commit/CI URLs, requirements and child-requirement states, authored proposal coverage separately from source metadata, company/title/person/source counts, image totals and unavailable records, historical metric coverage, calibration assumptions, UI/screens visited, test evidence and remaining limitations. Reconcile stale/contradictory reports through generated counts where possible. No declaration of perfect completion while mechanical requirements remain open.
