## Traceability contract

Maintain requirements.csv and input-effects.csv. Each requirement has ID, player action, persisted data, validation, reader/consumer, observable effect, lifecycle reversal, unit test, browser test where applicable, source or calibration status, implementation status and evidence commit. A field displayed on a page is not proof of a mechanical effect. Honest identity/document fields need no artificial money or quality bonus.

For every mechanical choice, demonstrate changing only that choice changes the intended outcome while unrelated state stays stable. Include debit/credit, costs, dates, tasks, availability, continuity or release consequences as relevant. Explain calibrated figures as game design. Keep factual research separate from simulated formula coefficients.

## Required acceptance suites

T01 Content: every actual genre ID has at least 100 primary-assigned reviewed original blueprints, at least 50 film and 50 TV. At least 14,900 globally unique IDs. No missing required blueprint fields, unresolved character/scene links, duplicate IDs or unreviewed quota entries. Export semantic-duplicate review findings, not just string inequality.

T02 Genre variety: curated supported pairings meet the coverage and ten-story rules; both genres influence story structure. Test obscure genres and cross-family combinations. Family-level templates are not counted as genre-specific authored stories without substantive differences.

T03 Blueprint editing: apply a film and series through UI; preview, preserve a custom field, undo, reload and continue. Verify characters, relationships, outline and episode progression populate together and obey constraints. Test fields from the former 11 gaps.

T04 Character lifecycle: add two characters, rename one, connect relationship/scene/look, cast a performer, change performer, split voice/body assignments, archive a referenced character with explicit reassignment, reload and verify contracts and receipts survive. Also test user-created characters without a blueprint.

T05 People: contrasting fictional profiles make different evidence-based decisions; one person working several roles cannot overlap commitments. Real-person fixtures keep sourced facts separate, reject future-credit leakage, and retain simulated work after permitted catalog refresh/removal. Producer and casting-director hires must perform paid tasks with observable results.

T06 Adaptation: catalog search -> source provenance -> option -> brief -> rewrite -> expiry/renewal or exercise -> production -> credits -> settlement. Test unknown rights, failed development and expired options without double charges. Keep fictional agreements distinct from real permissions.

T07 World: shoot away from base across time zones and holidays, with travel, availability, child-rule handling and a backup place. Changing a location after completed work preserves costs and opens only affected tasks. No impossible site guaranteed to exist merely because its country is in the registry.

T08 Finance/release: studio and distributor staggered releases, localization, admissions, receipts, recoupment, player earnings and history records reconcile. Retry events, reload and advance time: no duplicate settlements or double-counted worldwide totals.

T09 Audiences: linear live, delayed viewing, weekly streaming, batch streaming and daily/live shows retain distinct metrics; weekly reports lead to coherent continuation/cancellation and payment effects. Missing historical values never become invented real observations. A series completes season one and season two in the same browser career.

T10 Media: manifest audit for every imported entity; actual authorized sample fetch; interruption/resume, rate limit, missing image, wrong hash, storage refusal, removal and unauthorized access. Download-all totals are reported independently of fixture tests. Images do not inflate startup bundles or corrupt saves.

T11 UI: browser journeys on desktop Chromium, mobile Chromium and available iPhone 15 Plus/Pro Max WebKit profiles. Inspect screenshots and assert no unintended overflow, overlap, clipped controls or inaccessible dialogs; test keyboard navigation, labels, focus, zoom, long content and reduced motion. Real-device and human screen-reader checks remain explicitly unverified unless actually performed.

T12 Career: new game -> choose full blueprint -> edit/add characters -> secure financing -> cast/hire -> complete writing/design/production/post -> release -> weekly reports and ledger -> awards eligibility where supported -> future offers. Include a birthday during work and save/reload between major stages. Use public UI actions for journey evidence; fixture preparation must be disclosed and cannot substitute for the journey being claimed.

## Performance and completion

Measure content search, applying a large blueprint, character editing, images in a long list, pack install/resume and advancing a long career. Record before/after hardware/runtime, dataset size, median and tail latency, memory and bytes. Retain current enforced budgets; propose measured remediation rather than raising limits to hide regressions. Index and lazy-load large libraries; do not import 14,900 stories into startup JS.

Run existing checks, new meaningful unit/integration tests, mutation checks for key invariants, full development and production Playwright suites and seeded career runs. Separate engine-only seeds from UI playthroughs. Investigate nondeterminism rather than simply enlarging timeouts or deleting assertions.

Completion means implemented plus verified requirements and accurately counted content. Publish separate totals for authored stories, reviewed stories, sources, imported titles/people and downloaded images. A green CI does not prove historical completeness, originality or 100% realism. Blocked sources stay visible with a next retrieval route; do not fabricate values or block unrelated functional work.
