# Data Provenance

This is developer documentation. None of it is shown in normal gameplay.

| Data | Source | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Genre catalog (149 IDs, names, definitions, mechanic specifications) | Package `genres/registry.json` (from montrealroots/SLATE `src/person/genres/genres.json`, blob 82ff633d…). Copied unchanged to `src/data/genre-registry.json`, sha256 be894ee3…006f | The 169 inherited source references were **not** re-verified in this build. Definitions and mechanics are working design. |
| Genre-to-mechanic mapping and convention questions | `src/engine/genres.ts`, written for this build from each entry's mechanic specification | Design decision, not a historical fact. |
| Time zones and local time | IANA zone identifiers resolved by the runtime's `Intl` implementation | Accuracy follows the runtime's time-zone database. The world check recorded tz data 2026e in Node 22.23.4; browsers carry their own copy. |
| World gazetteer (252 entries, 3,865 regions, 171,750 places in 246 country shards) | SLATE-REBUILD snapshot 88256bfc (GeoNames export of 2026-10-26, donor register R-0032 to R-0034), copied to `src/world/gazetteer/` | GeoNames data, CC BY 4.0: "Data from GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/)". Structurally validated by `npm run world:check` (every row: unique IDs, coordinate bounds, names, IANA zones, region references; 0 problems). Not every settlement on Earth; GeoNames gives no accuracy guarantee. Six entries aren't playable (historical AN and CS; AQ, BV, HM and UM have no populated place); reasons in `docs/world/world-coverage.json`. Kosovo uses the user-assigned code XK with a neutral label. The ledger is `docs/world/COUNTRY-COVERAGE.csv`. |
| Original 66 catalog places | Hand-entered, now aliased to GeoNames records by `scripts/legacy-places.mjs` | 64 matched automatically (same country, name, under 15 km); 2 reviewed by hand as spelling variants (Marrakech = GeoNames "Marrakesh"; Panaji = "Panjim"). Original and candidate coordinates, distance and confidence are in `docs/world/legacy-place-migration.json`. Catalog IDs stay valid, so old saves keep working. |
| Climate normals | WMO climatological normals from the donor's compiled station file (11,459 stations; register R-0037/R-0038), copied to `src/world/weather/stations.json` | Per place and per normal, the nearest station within 400 km is used: own country 1991–2020 first, then own country any period, then anywhere (marked borrowed). Temperatures are height-corrected at 6.5 °C/km. Beyond 400 km, or where no station has a normal, that normal is **modeled** from latitude and height, and the provenance says so. Daily weather around the normals is the game's model, not an observation. 13 entries have no station of their own (`world-coverage.json`). |
| Map outlines | Natural Earth 1:110m via the donor (register R-0035), `src/world/map/world-map.json` | 175 outlines; 77 entries have none and are selected from the searchable list or coordinate markers instead. Public domain (Natural Earth terms). |
| Holiday source records | Donor research records per country, `docs/world/holiday-sources/` (218 files) | The donor's research dates and statuses are kept as they were; they are not re-verified by this build unless stated below. |
| Climate parameters per place | Approximate typical values chosen for the weather model | **Modeled.** Not station normals. Weather is never presented as an observation. |
| Daylight | Standard sunrise equation (solar declination and hour angle) | A deterministic calculation, accurate to a few minutes. Tested against London's solstice day length and polar day and night in Tromsø. |
| Public holidays | Rules written for each catalog country (fixed dates, nth weekdays, Easter offsets, observed substitutes, Japan's equinox formula and substitute rules, Korea's substitute rule) | Lunar and lunisolar observances come from the sourced 2026 tables below; later years come from the worldwide rules (next row) and are labelled projected or expected. Some regional holidays are covered (for example GB-ENG and GB-SCT, CA-ON, CA-BC, CA-QC, DE-BE, CH-TI, AU-NSW, AU-VIC). The rules were written from general knowledge and checked only by the tests in `tests/foundation.test.ts`. Italy's possible reinstatement of October 4 and New Zealand's Matariki dates were left out because they were not verified. |
| Worldwide holiday rules (218 entries, 2026-2100) | Donor rules `src/world/holidays/countries.json` (each country's rules read from its law or government list, per the donor's research records) and calendar tables `calendars.json` (Umm al-Qura, Hebrew, Chinese, Korean, Persian, Ethiopian; donor register R-0106, compile tool not in the package); equinoxes from `seasons.json` | Used for every country without curated rules, and for China and South Korea in years after their sourced tables, and for the Islamic feasts of curated countries in years without a sourced Eid table. Years past a government's last announcement are labelled projected; Islamic dates are labelled expected. 34 entries have no rules and say so in the game. Spot-checked by `tests/world-holidays.test.ts` (US substitute days, France, Japan's equinox days, Saudi Arabia, Poland, regional Scotland, China 2027 projected); not re-verified country by country. Names are kept in each country's official language. |
| China 2026 holidays and make-up working days | General Office of the State Council notice, November 4, 2025: https://www.news.cn/politics/20251104/88bcffd88ae249e58c699b8772548e3d/c.html | Official. Fetched November 6, 2026. Replaces the computed China rules for 2026 only. |
| South Korea 2026 lunar holidays (Seollal February 16–18, Buddha's Birthday May 24 with substitute May 25, Chuseok September 24–26) | https://publicholidays.co.kr/ (secondary), consistent with https://calendarific.com/holidays/2026/KR | Secondary sources that agree. Fetched November 6, 2026. |
| South Korea Labour Day and Constitution Day as public holidays from 2026 | https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/constitution-day-to-be-reinstated-as-public-holiday-starting-this-year/12427789 and https://en.fnnews.com/news/202604271733239058 | News reports of the law and Cabinet change. Fetched November 6, 2026. |
| Eid al-Fitr (March 20, 2026) and Eid al-Adha (May 27, 2026) for EG, MA, AE, SA, TR, NG, BF and KE | https://calendarific.com/holidays/2026/AE, https://www.khaleejtimes.com/ramadan/uae-likely-dates-for-hijri-new-year-next-ramadan-eid-al-fitr-eid-al-adha-revealed | **Expected** dates that depend on moon sighting; shown as expected in the game. Only the first day is included, although several countries observe more days. Years without a sourced table take the worldwide rules' Umm al-Qura dates, labelled expected. |
| Festival and award names | Planning roster in the package (`FOUNDATION-HISTORY.md`) | Real institutions. Their **rules in the game are simulated**; the event detail screen says so. |
| Official dates | 99th Academy Awards on March 14, 2027 and 100th on March 5, 2028 (https://press.oscars.org/news/academy-and-abc-announce-show-dates-99th-and-100th-oscarsr, cited in the package); FESPACO 2027 on March 20–27, 2027, entries February 24–October 20, 2026 (https://fespaco.bf/en/call-for-entries-fespaco-2027/, cited in the package) | Taken from the package's recorded source checks. They were not re-fetched in this build. Entry deadlines for the Oscars editions are simulated. |
| All other edition dates | Generated once per edition from a seasonal window and the save seed | **Simulated**, and labeled as simulated on the event screen. |
| Sundance location (Boulder, Colorado) | Public announcement of the festival's move from 2027 | From general knowledge; not re-verified in this build. |
| Heritage and language lists | Written for this build | Self-identification lists; players can describe their own. They have no effect on ability or outcomes. |
| People, distributors and rival films | Generated from word lists | Fictional. |
